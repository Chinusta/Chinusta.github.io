Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Özbekça

This policy explains what data the ÇinUsta (ChinUsta) mobile app ("the App") collects, how it is used and who can see it.

1. Data we collect

  • Profile: your name, phone number, role (customer or craftsman) and, for craftsmen, specialties.
  • Job posts: the work items, region and district, price, duration and your comment.
  • Exact address and location: the address you type when posting a job and, if you allow it, your device's GPS coordinates (once, for that job only). The App does not track your location in the background.
  • Technical identifier: an anonymous account ID created by Firebase Authentication.
  • Feedback: text you send in the "Suggestions" section. It is not linked to your account.

We do not collect ID documents, payment cards, contacts, photos or microphone data.

2. Why we use it

  • To show your job post to craftsmen and connect you with the craftsman who takes the job.
  • So the craftsman can reach the exact address.
  • To improve the App and fix bugs.

Your data is not used for advertising and is never sold.

3. Who can see it

  • The public part of a job post (work items, price, district, your name) is visible to other App users.
  • Your phone number and exact address are shown only to the one craftsman who takes the job. The customer likewise sees that craftsman's name and phone number.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf.
  • Competent authorities, where required by law.

4. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure that phone numbers and addresses can be read only by the job owner and the craftsman who took the job.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App: Profile → Delete account (Akkauntni öçiriş). Your profile, job posts and addresses are deleted immediately. If you no longer have the App, see Delete account.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
