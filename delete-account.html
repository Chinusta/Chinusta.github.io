Delete your ChinUsta account

Özbekça

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

Open the ÇinUsta app → Profil (bottom menu) → Akkauntni öçiriş (Delete account) at the bottom → Ha, öçirilsin (Yes, delete). Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: without the app

Email Solixon00@gmail.com with the name and phone number you used in the app. Your account will be deleted within 7 days and we will reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, role, specialties.
  • All job posts you created, with their exact address and GPS coordinates.
  • Your name and phone number from jobs you took as a craftsman; unfinished jobs are reopened for other craftsmen.

What is kept

  • Feedback messages sent via "Suggestions" — they are not linked to your name or phone number.
  • Your name may remain on jobs you took that the customer later cancelled; you can request its removal via Option 2.
